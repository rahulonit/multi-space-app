Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about safety, privacy, and performance.

Is PINGGO safe for my WhatsApp and Telegram accounts?

Yes, 100%. PINGGO loads the official web clients (web.whatsapp.com, web.telegram.org) inside standard native WebKit/WebView2 browser partitions. It does not use unauthorized reverse-engineered APIs, so your phone number is never at risk of being flagged or banned.

Does PINGGO send my chat messages to third-party servers?

No. PINGGO operates strictly on-device. When using PINGGO AI, transcripts are only sent directly to your selected provider (Google Gemini, OpenAI, or your own local Ollama instance). If you use the built-in offline smart engine, zero data leaves your Mac or PC.

Why is PINGGO faster and lighter than Franz, Ferdi, or Rambox?

Legacy apps bundle an entire Electron and Chromium runtime for every single window. PINGGO is written natively in Swift (macOS) and C# WinUI 3 (Windows), tapping directly into the operating system's built-in web engine for 4x lower RAM consumption.
